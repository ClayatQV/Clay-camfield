# Open questions from Clay's first review (Nov 19)

Things from the review that need a decision or a design conversation before building. Easy, clear fixes from the same review were made directly (see the commit "Review fixes from Clay's first run").

## Assessment and algorithm
1. **How long is long enough?** Clay: "it needs to be as long as it can be, or as short as it can be, but still long enough to get everything." Budget is now 22 + 12 + 9 screens (about 8 minutes). Do we let the coach keep going past that when it still has real gaps in its picture, with a hard cap (say 12 minutes)? Or hold the line and accept a less complete first picture, refined by practice data later?
2. **Know → apply → fluid per skill.** The rules now ask for this ladder explicitly. Should every area get all three steps (longer, more accurate) or only the areas that matter for this player's vision (shorter)?
3. **Skill map presentation.** Clay didn't like per-skill labels like "at tempo" and "can teach it" showing up when they weren't asked. Levels are now evidence-only. Open: does he want per-skill levels at all, or one overall picture per area ("chords: strong; applying harmony while improvising: the gap")? Or both, with the detail folded away?
4. **Player personality types.** Clay's favorite part was the type ("Soul-Jazz Melodic Pro"). He wants it to be more like a real personality test (Enneagram / Working Genius / StrengthsFinder) with named types and a write-up that says what you're good at, where you want to be, how to get there — not a restatement of answers. Needs a workshop: what are the types? What dimensions define them (melody-first vs harmony-first, feel vs technique, groove vs lead, explorer vs master, …)?
5. **Influences with autocomplete.** He wants to type influences and have names pop up (like a search), top 3–10, and for the coach to also suggest players he hasn't heard of. The free-text version is in; autocomplete needs a data source (an artist database or an AI lookup per keystroke). Worth it now or later?
6. **Experience and learning habits.** Now asked (toured / sessions / taught / church; lessons / YouTube / transcriptions / books; routine or not). Does this need its own short block, or is a few questions in Skills enough?
7. **"Not boxing players into a genre."** The single "what do you mostly play" question is gone; genres come ranked (up to 5, with Other typed). Is 5 enough, or should the rank be up to 10?

## Results and the plan
8. **Don't assume what to work on first.** The coach now asks "what's inspiring you right now, what do you want to work on first?" and the curriculum leads with it. Open: how much should the coach push back when what inspires you skips a real gap (e.g. wanting altered licks before chord tones)? Mention it, offer it as an optional module, or stay quiet?
9. **Module length in days, not just weeks.** Clay: "a week could be days." Options: keep 1–3 weeks (simple), or allow 3–21 days per module, or make a module "until done when is met." Which?
10. **"Done when" wording.** He didn't like it ("four original diminished licks memorized" felt weird). Keep done-when checks but phrase them as things you can hear yourself do? Replace with "you'll know it's working when…"? Drop them?
11. **Build page as its own mini-assessment.** The bigger idea: not just keep/swap/drop the coach's modules, but a builder where you choose what kinds of practice you want (technique drills, triads daily, transcriptions), minutes per kind, days per week per kind (e.g. drills 5 days, a song 2 days), and the coach fills the specifics. This is a real design piece; sketch it together before building.
12. **Specific videos, not YouTube searches.** Clay set the standard with three baseline videos (now in the app as approved lessons and in `api/_lib/transcriptions.ts`): Rens Warnar Music "Kid Charlemagne Solo with TAB", DW Scores "Satin Doll by Joe Pass - Transcription (w/Tabs)", François Leduc "Over the Rainbow - Ted Greene (Transcription)". The rule: the full recording or solo with tabs or notation on screen, synced to the audio, free. Module songs that match the library now link straight to the lesson page (video + looper); the coach marks each song as "transcription" or "by ear" and suggests an alternative with the same lesson value when a named piece has no such video (e.g. Eric Gales' Blackbird from an interview). Open: growing the library beyond three (hand-curated by Clay, or a YouTube Data API search with the AI judging "tabs on screen" and Clay approving in the Lessons review queue). Also: Spotify links should land on the artist/album page, which needs Spotify's API for IDs.
13. **Transcriptions as the core material.** "We gotta be able to learn stuff note for note." Beyond linking videos: do we build our own tabs/notation viewer synced to the video (the Kid Charlemagne bar map is a start), or lean on YouTube videos with tab on screen plus the A–B looper?
14. **Practice page flow.** *(Built Nov 21: guided mode, one part at a time, timers on demand, Lab inline; List mode kept.)* Original note: Clay wants it sequential (warm-up 1 → warm-up 2 → concept → application) without leaving the page, timers that start when you say so, and the Lab in view without losing your place. Drills now open inline; the timer already carries across pages. Open: a true step-by-step "guided session" mode (one part at a time, Next button, auto-advance at time) versus the current card list?
15. **Session length beyond 30/60.** *(Built Nov 21: Short / Full computed from the plan.)* Original note: Any minutes can be set on the Build page and parts scale, but the Practice toggle still shows 30/60. Replace the toggle with the plan's own length and a "short day" option?

## Product
16. **Gig setlist learning.** Import a Spotify playlist or a setlist and get help learning 40 songs fast (charts, keys, forms, the tricky parts). A separate feature; where does it sit on the roadmap?
17. **Free assessment as the front door.** Free assessment + profile online, "Build your practice routine," one free week or module, then pay. Shapes what needs accounts and payments. When?
18. **Speed.** Questions arrive in 6–13 s batches. Prefetching the next batch while the player answers isn't possible when questions depend on answers, but the coach could prepare two alternative next screens (yes-branch / no-branch) for yes/no questions. Worth the extra cost (roughly double per call)?
19. **Taste beyond artists.** He wants the taste profile to open doors: albums, not just artists (done), and recommendations he wouldn't find himself. How far should the coach go off his known names?
20. **Learn-by-ear guide when no transcription exists** *(First version built Nov 21 at `#/ear`; open: should every "ear" song get a guide automatically when the module is built, and when do we add audio-assisted drafts?)* (test case: Eric Gales' Blackbird, from an interview).** The coach cannot hear audio, so the honest version is: identify the underlying song (Blackbird), teach its skeleton from the original (form, key, the tenths), have the player mark section starts in the video, loop each section slowed with the Lab showing the chord tones, and give "what to listen for" per section as hypotheses. Later: an uploaded audio file through pitch detection and stem separation gives a rough draft to correct (works best on solo guitar). Decide: build the by-ear mode now as the fallback for every "ear" song, or wait until the library is bigger?

## To add (not yet started)
21. **Genre emphasis per module (Clay, Nov 22).** Two different things, decided:
    - **Listening: up to two genres per module**, for diversity. The listening picks for that stretch of weeks (Home "listen this week", Plan, Results) draw from both, with the overall taste profile as the fallback when a module has none.
    - **Material: one genre emphasis per module.** The coach recommends a genre for the module (e.g. a ♭9 module recommends blues for this player), but a **genre drop-down on the module** lets the player hear the same idea in any genre: pick "gospel" and the coach swaps in a use case of the ♭9 sound in gospel, and the transcription song changes with it. The concept stays, the vehicle changes.
    - **Reroll on any song.** If the player doesn't like the song chosen for the transcription, a "Pick a new song" button asks the coach for another in the same genre that teaches the same idea, keeping what they've already rejected out.
    - Build notes: `emphasis` (one genre) and `listening` (up to two genres) become fields on the module; the module writer, Swap and Add take them as hard constraints; the genre drop-down and the reroll call the single-module route with the concept, the genre and the rejected titles; rerolled songs that match the transcription library link straight to the lesson. Lab warm-ups stay neutral unless the genre has an obvious equivalent (shuffle feel, gospel passing chords). Open: does a genre change reset the module's warm-ups and done-when checks, or only the songs?
